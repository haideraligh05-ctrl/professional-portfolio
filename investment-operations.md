# Investment operations workflow MVP

**Aligh Nexus Solutions project · MVP implementation**

The MVP models the administrative work behind a digital investment platform. It covers investor onboarding, identity review, funding requests, investment requests, portfolio tracking, and reporting.

The archived implementation documents a Next.js frontend, an Express API, and PostgreSQL with Prisma. Manual review is central to the workflow. An operator reviews onboarding and funding requests before the user proceeds, and an approved investment request feeds the portfolio view.

**Demonstration scope.** The MVP uses recorded funding approvals and administrator-managed products and returns.
