# Salary validation workflow and benefits prototype

**Problem.** Manual eligibility checks made it difficult to apply validation rules consistently across consultant and manager roles and explain the outcome to reviewers.

**Work represented.** Business requirements for a salary validation system, a workflow prototype, and an offline reference for role-based eligibility and benefits calculations.

**Workflow.** Select the assessment period and role, validate the relevant performance inputs, calculate an eligibility outcome, and route exceptions for review with supporting evidence.

**Illustrative acceptance checks**

- Each role receives only the eligibility criteria applicable to it.
- Missing performance inputs generate a review item.
- Any revised decision preserves the original outcome, reason, and reviewer record.

**Stage.** Requirements for business and IT review, plus prototypes.
