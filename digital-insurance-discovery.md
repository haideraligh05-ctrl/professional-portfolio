# Digital insurance platform discovery

**Aligh Nexus Solutions project · Solution proposal**

An enterprise insurance platform brief covered customer, policy, billing, claims, and distribution workflows alongside operational controls and reporting.

Aligh Nexus proposed a discovery phase to validate requirements, define the first release, and establish acceptance criteria for phased implementation. The proposal starts with a narrow operating scope and groups later capabilities into separately reviewable increments.

**Deliverables proposed.** A prioritized scope, a build-ready requirements baseline, and a phased roadmap tied to demonstrations and acceptance milestones.
