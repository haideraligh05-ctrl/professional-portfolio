# Business operations dashboard requirements

**Problem.** Business performance, financial measures, and portfolio movements were spread across several MIS outputs and could not be reviewed consistently in one place.

**Work represented.** Business requirements for an executive dashboard covering sales, portfolio movement, persistency, targets, costs, and validation.

**Design.** Use common periods and reporting dimensions across source reports. Support drill-down by channel, product, and location, with exportable detail and reconciled totals.

**Illustrative acceptance checks**

- Period and scope selections apply consistently across the overview, drill-down, and export.
- Totals reconcile to the supporting records at each accessible reporting level.
- Portfolio movement and business production remain distinguishable on the same dashboard.

**Stage.** Business requirements and reporting design.
