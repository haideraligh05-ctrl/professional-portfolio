# Commercial approval and order tracking

**Aligh Nexus Solutions project · Local working prototype**

A commercial team needed a shared view of sales opportunities as they moved through technical review, order processing, and project delivery.

Aligh Nexus developed a browser-based Flask prototype with SQLite persistence and seeded demo data. The workflow brings sales, technical approvals, order tracking, delivery, and reporting into one workspace.

**Demonstration scenario.** Follow a sample opportunity from intake through technical review, then use its status to identify the next handoff before fulfillment. The archive documents local launch, tests, and backup and restore procedures.
