# Adviser performance and hiring analytics

**Problem.** Sales operations needed a repeatable way to compare hiring cohorts and understand adviser activity and production.

**Work represented.** A standalone dashboard and an agency hiring analysis, supported by workbook checks and dashboard validation scripts.

**Workflow.** Organize adviser-level reporting into cohorts, trace headline measures to their source, and present performance and hiring analysis for management review.

**Illustrative acceptance checks**

- Cohort counts reconcile to the underlying adviser population for the selected period.
- Unmapped adviser records are visible for review.
- Changing a reporting filter updates both the summary and underlying detail consistently.

**Stage.** Dashboard prototypes and analytical reporting.
